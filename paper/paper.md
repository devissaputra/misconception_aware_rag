# Does Wrong-Answer Conditioning Change Educational Evidence Retrieval? A Reproducible SciQ Study

## Abstract

Misconception-aware retrieval systems assume that a learner's incorrect response can usefully condition evidence retrieval, yet many prototypes evaluate only hand-authored examples. This study uses the real external SciQ benchmark to test a narrower and falsifiable question: whether adding an observed wrong-answer option to a science question changes BM25 retrieval of the item's supporting paragraph. Question-only retrieval is compared with wrong-answer-conditioned retrieval, while a gold-correct-answer condition is retained solely as an oracle upper bound. Retrieval quality is measured with rank-based metrics and paired uncertainty is resampled at the question level.

## Research question

How does conditioning a science evidence query on an observed wrong answer affect retrieval of the source supporting passage relative to question-only BM25?

## Data

The study uses the SciQ test split pinned at revision `2c94ad3e1aafab77146f384e23536f97a4849815`. The runner verifies the parquet SHA-256 before analysis. SciQ includes a question, three distractors, a correct answer and, for most items, a supporting paragraph.

## Conditions

1. **Question only:** BM25 query contains the question.
2. **Wrong-answer conditioned:** query contains the question and one distractor.
3. **Oracle corrective:** query additionally contains the gold correct answer and is interpreted only as an upper-bound sensitivity condition.

Every distractor creates one wrong-answer proxy case. Distractors are not treated as expert misconception labels.

## Evaluation

The corpus consists of unique non-empty support paragraphs in the test split. The relevant document is the support paragraph associated with the question. Metrics are MRR, Recall@1, Recall@3, Recall@5 and nDCG@5. The primary paired MRR difference is bootstrapped by question block to preserve dependence among a question's three distractor cases. Directional error analysis counts improvements, degradations and ties.

## Results

All numerical results are generated by `scripts/run_sciq_study.py` into `results/metrics.json`, `results/per_case_metrics.csv`, `results/summary.md` and `paper/results.md`.

## Relationship to the tutoring prototype

The repository also contains a cue-based misconception hypothesis detector, pedagogical reranker, evidence sufficiency logic, abstention and deterministic citation-grounded generation. Those components remain software research scaffolding. The SciQ experiment validates a retrieval-conditioning question, not the full diagnostic tutor.

## Limitations

Wrong answer options are not learner-produced misconception annotations. BM25 is lexical. The evidence corpus is formed from SciQ support paragraphs rather than a real course repository. Retrieval relevance does not establish explanation quality or learning benefit. The oracle condition is not deployable.
