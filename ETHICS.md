# Ethics and Responsible-Use Boundary

This repository must not convert an incorrect answer into a permanent or consequential learner label.

A wrong response may reflect a misconception, a slip, ambiguity, incomplete knowledge, language difficulty, alternative reasoning, inaccessible wording or random choice. SciQ distractors are therefore treated only as **wrong-answer proxies**.

## Main safeguards

- empirical claims use frozen external SciQ data rather than synthetic fixtures;
- distractors are never called validated learner misconceptions;
- generated per-case evidence does not redistribute SciQ question/support text;
- the shuffled condition is explicitly a lexical-expansion control, not a simulated learner response;
- the gold correct answer appears only in an oracle-informed sensitivity condition;
- the prototype uses *possible misconception* language rather than certain diagnosis;
- abstention remains part of the prototype response path;
- retrieval quality is separated from pedagogical effectiveness.

## Negative-control interpretation

The shuffled wrong-answer control intentionally breaks the relationship between question and appended distractor text while preserving the distractor-text distribution. It can help distinguish content-specific conditioning from generic query-expansion effects, but it is not a complete causal identification strategy for learner cognition.

## Learner-facing research requirements

Before testing diagnostic claims with real learners, a study would need consent/privacy governance, expert misconception annotation, adjudication, held-out learners, analysis of slips and ambiguous responses, accessibility review, teacher oversight, subgroup assessment and a policy preventing speculative hypotheses from becoming durable learner profiles.

## Deployment boundary

Retrieval success, citations and abstention logic do not establish instructional safety or learning benefit. Any learner-facing deployment would require pedagogical and human-subject evaluation beyond this repository.
