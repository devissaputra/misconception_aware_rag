# Ethics and Responsible-Use Boundary

This repository must not turn an incorrect answer into a permanent learner label.

A wrong response may reflect a misconception, a slip, ambiguity, incomplete knowledge, language difficulty, alternative reasoning, inaccessible wording or random choice. SciQ distractors are therefore treated only as wrong-answer proxies.

## Main safeguards

- empirical claims are based on external SciQ data, not synthetic fixtures;
- the external study never calls distractors validated misconceptions;
- the gold correct answer appears only in an explicitly labeled oracle sensitivity condition;
- generated per-case metrics do not redistribute the source question/support text;
- the prototype detector uses the phrase *possible misconception*;
- abstention is part of the prototype response path;
- retrieval quality is kept separate from pedagogical effectiveness.

## Learner-facing research requirements

Before testing diagnostic claims with real learners, a study would need appropriate consent/privacy governance, expert annotation guidance, adjudication, held-out evaluation, analysis of slips/ambiguity, accessibility review, teacher oversight and a plan preventing speculative hypotheses from becoming durable learner profiles.
